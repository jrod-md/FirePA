# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

FirePA serves a research-literate public reader, a technical reader who wants
to inspect the FIRMS-to-cluster-to-optical narrowing, and an auditor who needs
stable public files, citations, provenance, and explicit boundaries. The site
must work as a long-form research story first, with deeper methodology and
reproducibility surfaces available when needed.

## Product Purpose

FirePA is a static public research experience for a frozen remote-sensing
pilot in Coclé, Panamá. It makes the local evidence chain inspectable while
keeping provisional thermal events, descriptive measurements, external
relationship checks, and unresolved limitations separate from confirmed-fire,
severity, prediction, or operational claims.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, rigorous, restrained. The voice should feel like an annotated field
report: confident about what the package contains, precise about what it does
not establish, and never promotional or alarmist.

## Anti-references

Avoid wildfire-alert dashboards, SaaS metric cards, emergency-control-room
styling, heat-intensity color scales, fake satellite interfaces, generic
beige marketing layouts, and interactions that imply spread, certainty, or
real-time monitoring.

## Design Principles

- Put evidence before atmosphere.
- Make every narrowing step and denominator legible.
- Keep caveats adjacent to the claim they qualify.
- Treat frozen figures and public records as inspectable evidence, not raw
  material for decorative reinterpretation.
- Use interaction to clarify relationships, never to manufacture drama.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic landmarks and heading order, visible keyboard focus, a skip
link, text alternatives for maps and figures, accessible event selection and
table/list fallbacks, explicit UTC units and status words, readable wide-data
overflow, and reduced-motion behavior that preserves the same content and
focus order.
