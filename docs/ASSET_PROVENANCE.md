# FirePA decorative asset provenance

## `site/public/assets/mineral-field.png`

This PNG is a non-scientific material surface created specifically for the
FirePA visual experience and supplied as an authored/generative artwork asset.
It is used only as restrained surface treatment behind the Hero and Chapter 01
frontend compositions.

The asset does not represent Coclé, terrain, satellite imagery, measured
mineral composition, contours, coordinates, thermal detections, or any other
scientific observation. It must not be interpreted as evidence or a geographic
layer.
